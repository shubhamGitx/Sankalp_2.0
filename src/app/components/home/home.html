ABOUT THE MISSION

Building a Sustainable Bihar

Sankalp Setu is an initiative focused on water conservation, environmental protection, increasing green cover and promoting sustainable development across Bihar.

The mission brings together citizens, departments and local bodies to create a greener and more sustainable future.
